# WP18 — Evidence registry and boundary criteria (the research layer of v3)

Fresh session (`/clear`). No user interface in this WP: it builds the data the v3 interface and
the presentation will show.

<context>
- `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/research/v3-evidence-report.md` (**the research report: 35 evidence
  candidates E01–E35, market scan, survey design**), `docs/design/v2-spec.md` (§2 principles, §3
  decision model), `docs/spec/model.md` §10–§11, `docs/reports/WP17.md`
- skills `adr`, `wp-report`

The supervisor reframed the project: solid research first; the application is a prototype of how
such a tool could work; the core question is **Human in the Loop — where may AI work in a company
and its management, where must a human stay, where is the boundary of "taste"; fewer or more
tools?** Version 3 answers with six modules (Raporty, Projekty, Finanse, Ludzie, Uprawnienia AI,
Mapa granic) and an **always-visible "Dowód" point at every feature** that opens a short excerpt of
a source with the key phrase highlighted, its stance (wspiera / podważa / niuansuje), a summary of
what the researchers did and one sentence on what follows for Sentinel. The same registry feeds
the PDF presentation, which is what is graded. **A single invented or misquoted sentence in front
of the supervisor would be fatal**, so every quote must be checked against the source text by
code.
</context>

<task>
1. **Registry model**: `src/sentinel/evidence/` with Pydantic models and a loader for
   `docs/research/evidence/registry.json` (JSON, no new dependency). Fields per item: `id`
   (E01…), `title_pl` (short), `modules` (raporty | projekty | finanse | ludzie | uprawnienia |
   mapa), `criteria` (1–6 as in the report), `decisions` (ids from task 3), `stance` (wspiera |
   podwaza | niuansuje), `quote` (verbatim, language of the source, at most 3 sentences),
   `quote_pl` (only for legal texts: the official Polish wording), `highlights` (substrings of the
   quote to mark), `gloss_pl` (one line), `location` (page or section), `citation` (APA-like),
   `doi`, `url`, `open_access`, `licence`, `summary_pl` (2–3 sentences: method, sample, key result
   with numbers), `implication_pl` (one sentence: what follows for Sentinel), `verification`
   (`status`: verified | summary_only; `source_file` name, `sha256` of the fetched source, `method`,
   `checked_on`).
2. **Fetch and verify**: `scripts/fetch_evidence.py` downloads every open-access source of the
   registry (arXiv, PMC, publisher OA, author copies, OWASP, NIST, and **EUR-Lex official texts of
   Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 and of the GDPR, in English
   and Polish**) into `data/evidence/` (gitignored, never committed), extracts plain text
   (a dev-only extraction dependency is allowed; record it in an ADR), and writes the sha256. A
   `@pytest.mark.data` test asserts that every `verified` quote occurs in its extracted source text
   after a documented normalisation only (whitespace, line-break hyphenation, ligatures, curly vs
   straight quotes) and that every highlight occurs in its quote; it skips cleanly without data.
   - Re-verify the report's ✔ items; for the ✖ items (E17, E24, E26, E28–E33) find the exact
     passage in the open source and quote it, or set `summary_only` with no quote.
   - Paywalled sources: no quote unless the owner supplies the PDF (list them in the report with
     what to download from the university library); keep the summary.
   - Check the legal facts against the Official Journal: application dates after 2026/1744
     (Annex III obligations, Article 4 wording, Article 5 in force since 2 February 2025).
   - Never write a quote from memory or from a summary (including the report's own wording)
     without finding it in the fetched text.
3. **Decision catalogue**: `docs/research/decisions.md` — every design decision the user sees,
   with an id (D01…), one sentence, the screen and feature it belongs to, and its evidence ids.
   Cover v2 (dot grid "N na 100", states and thresholds, human approval of every change, validity
   date and cost of delay, contrastive reasoning written by code, correction "Popraw", rejection
   reasons, decision archive, no performance metrics, no absence-frequency question, voice with
   confirmation, local vs cloud model) and the v3 modules (tracked changes and fact guard in
   Raporty; audience versions; Finanse summary without approval rights; the "deliberately absent"
   features in Ludzie; LLM without tools in Uprawnienia AI; the boundary map; **a "Twoja ocena"
   step before Sentinel's recommendation** (cognitive forcing, E07) — mark this one as proposed).
   Every decision has at least one evidence item; mark decisions whose only evidence is
   theoretical.
4. **Boundary criteria and map**: `docs/research/boundary-criteria.md` — the six criteria with a
   definition, a test question, evidence ids, and the honest note that criterion 5 ("smak") is
   normative; and the process map: the 12 company processes of the survey (report editing,
   department versions, meeting summary, status messages to data, deadline forecast, choosing a
   corrective action, budget summary, approving a budget shift, evaluating an employee, choosing a
   substitute, a message to the team about a hard decision, a strategic decision) × the criteria,
   each with a zone (AI samodzielnie / AI z człowiekiem / tylko człowiek), the reason and the
   evidence ids. Also `docs/research/boundary-map.json` for the interface.
5. Our own evidence: add registry items for Sentinel's results (RQ1–RQ5 numbers from
   `generated/numbers.tex`, with the run ids and the number of runs), stance and caveats, so the
   interface can show "nasze badanie" next to the literature.
6. Version 1.9.0 (no interface change), zip from a clean tree (do not tag), `wp-report`
   (`docs/reports/WP18.md`) with: a table of all items (id, stance, status), what could not be
   verified and why, the list of PDFs the owner should get, and the legal dates as checked.
</task>

<constraints>
- No change to research semantics; `reproduce --check` stays 117/117.
- No new runtime dependency. Sources are never committed; only ids, hashes, short quotes (at most
  3 sentences each, attributed: Polish copyright law Art. 29 and 34) and our own summaries.
- No invented quotes, no paraphrase presented as a quote, no quote from memory.
- Default `pytest -q` ≤ 90 s. Do not restart the public demo.
- Never wait for a process with `pgrep -f <name>` in a shell loop.
</constraints>

<acceptance_criteria>
1. Every `verified` item passes the quote test against its fetched source; every other item is
   `summary_only` with a reason.
2. At least 25 items, at least 2 per module and per criterion, with both supporting and
   challenging evidence for modules Raporty, Projekty and Mapa granic.
3. Every decision in the catalogue has evidence; every cell of the process map has a zone, a
   reason and evidence.
4. Quality gate, `-m "data or slow"`, `reproduce --check` pass; 1.9.0 zip; pushed; clean tree.
</acceptance_criteria>
