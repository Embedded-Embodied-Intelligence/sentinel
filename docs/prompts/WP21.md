# WP21 — Calm screens: less on each screen, the research one click away; panel scroll; no dashes

Fresh session (`/clear`). Last interface WP before the defence (live demo is the presentation).

<context>
- `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/design/v3-spec.md`, `docs/design/v2-spec.md` (§2 principles: one screen at
  a glance, about four blocks above the fold), `docs/ui/v3/`, `docs/reports/WP19.md`,
  `docs/reports/WP20.md`
- skills `adr`, `wp-report`

The owner clicked through 2.0.2 and judged: **too much happens on every screen**; long "—" pauses
typical of AI-written text everywhere; and the Dowód panel's content scrolls with the whole page and
gets cut off at the bottom instead of scrolling inside the panel. The goal is a balance: operational
screens light and calm, the research always one click away (the Dowód points stay visible, but
quiet), and density accepted only where it is the content (Mapa granic, Źródła) — and even there
with a compact default.
</context>

<task>
1. **Panel scroll bug**: the Dowód panel (and any side panel) is fixed to the viewport, full
   height, scrolls inside itself (`overflow-y: auto`, `overscroll-behavior: contain`), the page
   behind does not scroll while it is open, nothing is cut off at the bottom at 1440×900 and
   1280×800; the same for Źródła's expanded items. e2e test that scrolls a long panel to its end.
2. **No "—" in our text**: remove the em dash (and the spaced en dash used as a pause) from every
   interface string (templates, translations PL and EN), from the registry's own fields
   (`title_*`, `gloss_*`, `summary_*`, `implication_*`), decisions, map reasons and criteria, and
   the user guides; rewrite with a full stop, colon, comma or parentheses. **Never touch verbatim
   quotes.** En dashes stay only in numeric ranges ("1–5", "9–16 lis"). A test fails on any "—"
   or " – " outside quotes and ranges.
3. **Text budget** for every v2/v3 screen (record it in v3-spec as §10 and enforce what can be
   tested): a heading at most 6 words; at most one helper sentence of at most 20 words per block;
   no paragraph longer than 3 lines at 1280 px; explanations that do not fit move into the Dowód
   panel or behind "Więcej". Rewrite or cut helper texts accordingly (plain language rules stay).
4. **Quieter Dowód points**: a small grey outline "ⓘ" (count shown on hover and focus, and in the
   accessible name), accent colour only on hover/focus; **at most one point per block** (merge the
   decisions of a block into one panel listing them); keep deep links working.
5. **A shorter panel**: on top a one-line summary ("3 dowody: 2 wspierają, 1 podważa"); each
   evidence card collapsed by default to stance, title, the quote with highlights and the gloss;
   "Co zrobili badacze" and "Co z tego wynika dla Sentinela" behind "Szczegóły badania" (one click,
   remembered while the panel is open); the first challenging item always visible (do not hide
   the counter-evidence).
6. **Screen by screen, at most about four blocks above the fold at 1280×800**, the rest collapsed
   or one level down, decided by a look-and-fix loop with screenshots (before and after pairs in
   the report):
   - Pulpit: verdict, three numbers, decision; "Zapas czasu", "Najbliższe 7 dni", "Przyczyny
     zmiany", "Główne ryzyka" as collapsible sections, the first two open, remembered per browser.
   - Mapa granic: compact default — one row per process with its zone and the deciding criteria
     as small icons; a switch "Pokaż wszystkie kryteria" for the full grid; cells icon-and-colour
     only with the word in the accessible name and tooltip; the process column wide enough that
     names do not wrap into five lines; the six questions as a compact legend (collapsible).
   - Raporty: the fact guard shows only "zmienione", "pominięte" and "nowe" by default, with
     "bez zmian: N" collapsed; shorter helper texts.
   - Decyzja, Raport, Finanse, Uprawnienia AI, Ludzie, Źródła, Start, Dziennik, Plan,
     Laboratorium, Ustawienia: the same budgets; fewer chips and badges; one primary action.
7. Tests: the dash test, the panel scroll e2e, page tests updated, the accessibility scan (light
   and dark, panel open), the presentation-path e2e; new screenshots in `docs/ui/v3/` (PL and EN,
   light, 1440×900 and 1280×800) and before/after pairs for Pulpit, Mapa granic, Raporty, Decyzja
   and the panel.
8. Version 2.1.0, zip from a clean tree (do not tag), `wp-report` (`docs/reports/WP21.md`).
</task>

<constraints>
- No change to research semantics or to any number; `reproduce --check` stays 128/128.
- Never edit a verbatim quote or its highlights; the registry's quote test must still pass.
- No new runtime dependency; labels per the glossary rules (headings nouns, buttons verbs).
- Default `pytest -q` ≤ 90 s on this server. Do not restart the public demo.
- Never wait for a process with `pgrep -f <name>` in a shell loop.
</constraints>

<acceptance_criteria>
1. The panel scrolls inside itself and nothing is cut off; the page behind stays put.
2. No "—" anywhere in our text (test), quotes untouched.
3. Every screen meets the text budget and shows at most about four blocks above the fold at
   1280×800 (screenshots); Dowód points are quiet and at most one per block; the panel opens
   short with details one click away.
4. Quality gate, `-m "data or slow"`, `-m e2e`, `reproduce --check` pass; 2.1.0 zip; pushed;
   clean tree.
</acceptance_criteria>
