The supervisor wants the project judged as research on Human in the Loop in companies; the app is
a prototype. WP18 built a verified evidence registry (52 items), a catalogue of 24 decisions and a
boundary map. This WP makes the interface show that research: an always-visible "Dowód" point at
every feature, and four screens of the "Zasady AI" group. WP20 builds Raporty, Finanse and the
RQ6 experiment.
</context>
