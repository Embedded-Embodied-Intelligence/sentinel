# WP16 notes — interface v2, part 2 (material for the article and the course report)

**Scope.** WP16 completes the ten screens of `docs/design/v2-spec.md`: Dziennik (§8.6), Plan
(§8.7), Laboratorium (§8.8), Kalendarz (§8.9) and Ustawienia (§8.10), plus the restyled per-task
form of the Raport. Nothing in the research core changed (`sentinel reproduce --check`: 117 of
117 files identical); every figure is one of spec §11.4–§11.8.

**The log as the record of a human-overseen system.** Every entry of the Dziennik comes from the
event log and names its actor ("Ty" for reports, decisions and corrections, "Sentinel" for plans,
recommendations, validity and expiry), so the page doubles as the record-keeping the EU AI Act
asks of a high-risk system (Art. 12) and shows the human oversight of Art. 14. A decision entry
expands to the brief as the manager saw it (chance, end and budget before and after, validity
date, cost of delay) and its fate with the reason. "Szansa w czasie" plots the chance in 100
after each report and approved decision, in log order (report dates, not storage times, label
the dots), and each dot opens its entry.

**The plan without performance metrics.** The Gantt chart draws the current residual plan (the
deterministic forecast of spec §5.2, §11.4), the forecast's band from the median to the 90th
percentile finish, and marks the tasks of the threat list (duration sensitivity, §11.4) in amber
with an icon and a word. The team view shows workload, never performance (Goodhart 1975;
Edmondson 1999; AI Act Annex III point 4): for each working day `d` of next week and role `k`,
`need_k(d)` is the people the active tasks of the plan need and `present_k(d)` those of the role
not absent; a person present on `d` gets `min(1, need_k(d) / present_k(d))` of a day, summed and
rounded ("ok. 5 z 5 dni"). It is the plan's demand on the role, equal for everyone in it.

**What if.** "Co jeśli?" runs the Raport's dry run (§11.8) on a scenario dated at the latest
report, so no working day passes and only the scenario moves the forecast. By spec §11.3 an
absence that starts after the report's day `t` waits for a later report, so the form pre-fills
"Od" with `date(t)` and says when an absence starts later. Showcase: Anna absent 16–23 Nov
lowers the chance from 39 to 14 in 100; with "Integracja płatności" two days longer and a new
three-day task as well, to 3. "Dodaj do raportu" carries the items as ticked cards.

**Simulator for the board.** Sentinel and NoReaction run on the same world (common random
numbers, the WP2b scenario seed); the page ends with one sentence. Small example, many
disruptions: "Sentinel dotrzymał terminu; bez reakcji te same zdarzenia opóźniły projekt o 21
dni roboczych" (9 plan changes and 4 temporary people, cost +32 units, +1 %).

**Calendar feed.** RFC 5545, written with the standard library: all-day `DATE` events, the weekly
reminder at a floating local time with `RRULE:FREQ=WEEKLY`, stable UIDs, lines folded at 75
octets. The link's token is the first 128 bits of HMAC-SHA256(server key, project and a stored
144-bit nonce), so a database copy holds no working link and a new nonce revokes all old ones
(ADR-0035). The feed holds names and dates only — the model has no absence reason to leak
(GDPR Art. 9, invariant 7) — and the page warns that anyone with the link sees them.

**Plain-language settings.** The AI model is chosen as "Chmura (zalecany) — dokładniejszy" or
"Lokalny — dane nie wychodzą z firmy, wolniejszy" (ISO 24495-1 plain language); keys are entered
only in an installed copy, never in the public demo, and never shown again.

**Hotfix (2026-11-08).** Contrastive explanations work best when the foil is a real
alternative the reader would think of (Miller 2019), so "Uzasadnienie" now contrasts the best
option of another kind of action (substitution, temporary person, dropping a task, a plan change
alone) and never the same action with a re-plan, which reads as the same choice. When that
option is within the materiality threshold of the chance (`ε_D` = 0.02, spec §6), the page says
that it would give a similar result and why the choice stands: as the simpler option, or by the
smaller expected delay when that differs by at least `ε_T` = 0.5 days. Decision titles name the
people by first name with the person stood in for declined by the regular Polish endings
("Cezary zastępuje Bartosza w „API katalogu produktów”", 52 characters, at most 60), plain
language over system wording (ISO 24495-1). A report card says when an absence that starts
after the report's day starts to count (spec §11.3). On this server a connection to 127.0.0.1
reaches the app from eth0's address, so "the local proxy" became "this machine", checked per
request through the routing table.
