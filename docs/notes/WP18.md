# WP18 notes — the evidence registry and the boundary map (material for the presentation, the article and the course report)

**Question.** The supervisor reframed the project around Human in the Loop: where may AI work in
a company and its management, where must a person stay, where is the boundary of "taste"? WP18
builds the evidence for the answer; the v3 interface and the PDF presentation read it.

**Registry.** `docs/research/evidence/registry.json`, 52 items, read by `sentinel.evidence`
(Pydantic, frozen). Kinds: 17 empirical studies, 11 theoretical texts (theory, reviews of ideas,
a vision), 9 legal provisions, 7 standards (OWASP, NIST, W3C, ISO), 2 vendor texts, 6 results of
Sentinel's own study (RQ1–RQ5). Stances: 37 support (wspiera), 11 nuance (niuansuje), 4 challenge
(podważa). Each item: modules, criteria, decisions, quote (at most three sentences; legal
quotes in English with the official Polish wording), highlights, gloss, location, APA-like
citation, DOI/URL, licence, summary (method, sample, result with numbers) and one sentence on
what follows for Sentinel.

**Verification method.** Quotes were copied only from the text extracted from the fetched
source (never from the research report or from memory) and are checked by code: a quote must
occur in the source text after a documented normalisation only (Unicode NFC, ligatures, curly
quotes, line-break hyphenation, white space; no case folding, dashes or punctuation). 40 of 40
quoted items pass, and the 8 official Polish wordings of the EU provisions with them. Abstracts of three paywalled
articles (Noy & Zhang, Lee & See, Galesic et al.) come from their PubMed records, which hold the
publisher's abstract. EU law comes from the Official Journal through the Publications Office
(Cellar); EUR-Lex's own site presented a wrong certificate on the day. Our own items are checked
against `paper/generated/numbers.tex` (macro values, run ids from `configs/paper.toml`).

**Our own evidence against our own design.** E52: in the RQ4 autonomy study, Sentinel that
asks approval for every change (simulated approval after one day) finished on time in 22.5%
of runs, the fully autonomous one in 33.8%, no reaction 21.2% (240 worlds per level). Human
approval has a cost in time; Sentinel shows the validity date and the cost of delay to keep it
small. E51: LLM proposals win 9.3% of decision states against 8.7% for a random control
(intervals overlap), so the LLM never chooses.

**Legal dates (Official Journal).** Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 of 8 July 2026, OJ L 24.7.2026,
in force on the third day after publication (27.7.2026). Chapter III Sections 1–3 apply from
2 December 2027 for Annex III systems and 2 August 2028 for Annex I systems (originally

prohibitions of Art. 5(1)(ba), (bb), (1a), (1b) from 2 December 2026; Art. 5(1)(f) (emotions at

development of AI literacy".

**Boundary map.** Six criteria (reversibility and stakes; verifiability; language work versus
numbers and judgment; impact on people and money; values, relations and taste; data
sensitivity). Criterion 5 is normative: its evidence is theory. Twelve processes of the survey;
a process takes the strictest zone of its six cells. Result: one process "AI samodzielnie"
(deadline forecast, computed by simulation), seven "AI z człowiekiem" (report editing,
department versions, meeting summary, status messages to data, choosing a corrective action,
budget summary, choosing a substitute), four "tylko człowiek" (approving a budget shift,
evaluating an employee, a message about a hard decision, a strategic decision).

**Decisions.** 24 decisions (D01–D24), each with evidence; D21 ("Twoja ocena" before the
recommendation, cognitive forcing, E07) is proposed. Basis from verified evidence: 16
empirical, 8 normative (laws and standards), none theoretical only.
