# WP21 notes: calm screens

Material for the report's chapter on the interface (v3 spec section 10, ADR-0039).

## Why

After clicking through 2.0.2 the owner judged that every screen showed too much at once. v2 spec
section 2 already rested on Few (2006, one screen at a glance), Cowan (2001, about four items in
working memory) and Sweller (1988, cognitive load), but no test held the screens to it, and the v3
research layer had added a point to almost every line. WP21 keeps the research one click away
and makes the operational screens light.

## The budget as implemented

| Rule | Where it is checked |
| --- | --- |
| Heading of the interface ≤ 6 words | `tests/test_v2_text.py`: 90 catalogue texts used in `h1`–`h3` of the v2 templates |
| One helper sentence ≤ 20 words per block | the same test: 110 texts used as a lead, a hint or a muted note |
| No paragraph over 3 lines at 1280 px | `tests/test_v2_e2e.py`, 22 screens at 1280×800, text visible without a click |
| ≤ 4 blocks of content above the fold | the same e2e test (heading and navigation aside; a block counts when it starts 48 px above the fold) |
| No dash as a pause | `tests/ui_check.py` on every rendered page; `tests/test_v2_text.py` on catalogues, messages, the registry's own fields, decisions, the map and both user guides |

A range keeps its en dash when both sides are numbers or dates ("1–5", "pt. 13 lis – pt. 20
lis"); a missing value is the word "brak" / "none" instead of a lone dash. 41 catalogue entries
were rewritten (the dashes out, the helpers down to one sentence); their meaning is unchanged, and
the research data's figures, quotes and highlights were not touched (the registry's own fields
held only numeric ranges).

## The evidence layer

- One point per block. The decisions of a block merge into one point (`D05+D24`); N is the
  number of distinct items of those decisions. Uprawnienia AI went from 12 points to 4, Ludzie
  from 6 to 2, Finanse from 3 to 2.
- The point is a 26 px grey outline; N appears on hover and focus (a bubble drawn from
  `data-n`, so not page text) and stays in the accessible name.
- The panel: a one-line count ("3 dowody: 2 wspierają, 1 podważa"); cards collapsed to stance,
  title, quote and gloss; "Szczegóły badania" opens the details of every card at once and holds
  while the panel is open. Above three items the panel shows the first item of each stance, so
  a challenging item is never hidden. D03's panel (9 items) went from 7,092 px to 1,383 px.
- The scroll bug: the modal `<dialog>` had the browser's `overflow: auto`, so it scrolled
  together with its body and passed the scroll to the page (measured: the page moved 686 px at
  1440×900, the panel's end out of view). Now the dialog is a flex column with `overflow:
  hidden`, its body the only scroller with `overscroll-behavior: contain`, and
  `html:has(.dowod[open])` locks the page.

## Screens

- Pulpit: verdict, three numbers and the decision above the fold; four sections fold, the
  first two open, the choice kept in `localStorage` (the default when storage is blocked).
- Mapa granic: one row per process with its zone and its deciding criteria (the cells whose
  zone equals the process's, by the strictest-zone rule) as numbered marks that open the cell's
  panel; the full grid on request, icon and colour only with the word in the name and tooltip.
- Decyzja: header, effect, reasoning, cost of delay and the actions fit 1280×800; "Kogo dotyczy"
  moved into "Szczegóły".
- Raporty: the fact guard shows changed, omitted and new facts; "bez zmian: N" is folded.
- Elsewhere: one primary action per screen, filters in one row (Źródła), the project as a list
  (Dziennik), the modes beside the heading (Laboratorium).
