# WP12 notes — one set of numbers in the application

Material for the course report (application chapter); the research core (§1–§10) is unchanged and
`sentinel reproduce --check` stays 117 of 117.

**Promise = result (spec §11.5, v2.2.0).** A live project's comparison no longer evaluates an
option as the rollouts of §6 do (the simulated human approves `δ_h = 1` day later in each sampled
world, the plan part re-planned there). Instead `ApprovalEvaluator` applies the option at once on
day `t` as the approval does (`approve`, `LiveProject.resolve_action`), builds the observation this
leaves, and runs the forecast's own rollouts from it on the forecast's seed and layout. The figures
a card promises are then, by construction, those the overview shows after the approval: the test
asserts equality of the chance, the 50/90 % quantiles of `C` and the mean cost. B3 of the CEO/QA
test had two causes. The imagined approval delay put the substitution at 55 % instead of 59 %
applied at once. The re-sampled worlds put it at 46 %: the observation lists a growth reported at
a check-in and a later handover at the same work done, and the replay of §5.1 puts a handover
before a growth at equal work, so the onboarding of 2 crew-member days became 5 (factor 2.5). In
research runs this tie cannot occur, because a growth is drawn in step 5 and the task progresses
the same day; it is a spec question for §5.1.

**Plan shown (§11.4).** The pages show the deterministic forecast of §5.2 from the latest
observation (nominal world, no future disruptions, NoReaction), its unstarted part checked by
`validate_residual` on the planning view of §4.1. It replaces the stale planned starts, which can
lie in the past and ignore new tasks' dependencies (B2).

**Causes of a move (§11.4).** For a move of at least 5 points at a check-in: one-at-a-time
counterfactuals of the check-in (each absence, growth and new task left out; the task reports
replaced by the deterministic forecast's progress since the previous check-in), each forecast on
the `N = 200` worlds of the check-in's day (common random numbers), at most three, ranked by
`|p − p_without|`, in the background job of the threats. Cost: one forecast per event, 0.1–0.4 s in
total on the showcase.

**Calendar (§11.1).** Polish public holidays (Gregorian computus for Easter, Meeus 1991; 24
December from 2025) and company days off; projects stored before 1.4.0 keep Monday–Friday (a
migration in `events.parse`), the simulator and research keep §10.1. The calendar is fixed once
tracking starts, since stored days are counted in it.

**AI in the application (ADR-0029).** The message parser of §10.2 is wrapped, not changed: the app's
schema adds `unparsed` (clause numbers) and `questions` (unknown person, ambiguous task), so the
cache and the jobs table hold no text of the message (invariant 7); prompt aids give the return day
by weekday and by date. Explanations are written in the page's language from an English addendum
and checked against the pages' list of internal terms (one retry, then the template alone). The
WBS extraction of RQ1 is wrapped likewise (optional flag, a short name with its sentence kept,
calendar facts). Live checks with `openai/gpt-oss-120b` (0.0049 USD) and `qwen2.5:7b`:
`docs/qa/ai/wp12-*.json`. The 7B model still gave an unknown person's absence to a team member, so the
review shows an absence whose person the message does not name unticked.

**Revised estimates and corrections (§11.2, §11.3).** A growth of a task not started revises its
estimate in the definition (`p_j + N`, or `p_j F`, rounded half up, at least `p_j + 1`). The forecast
then samples it around the new estimate. Tasks not started can be corrected after the first
check-in. The default budget is pinned at the first check-in, so neither moves it.
