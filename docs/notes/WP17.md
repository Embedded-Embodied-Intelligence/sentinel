# WP17 notes — fixes from the audit (material for the article and the course report)

**Scope.** The owner's audit of 1.7.0 (2026-11-08) found no failing page (515 pages crawled in
PL and EN, 27 page types in Chromium at 1280 and 1440 px without a script error, a failed
request or clipped text) but ten shortcomings, mostly of the public demo. WP17 fixes all ten;
release 1.8.0. Research results are unchanged (`sentinel reproduce --check`: 117 of 117 files).

**Announced absences (spec v2.4.1, §11.3).** Until 1.7.0 an absence reported for a later day
waited for the report that observed its first day, so "Anna is on holiday from the 17th",
reported on the 13th, left the chance at 39 in 100. Now the observation lists it as
`(w, s, L_low)`; the belief draws its true length `L ≥ L_low` from the same posterior as an
absence under way (§5.1), after every other draw of the world, and every rollout starts it on
day `s` (the absence that ends later counts if the person is already away; a D1 onset of an
absent worker is skipped). The forecast, the recommendations and "Co jeśli?" see it at once:
the same report now gives 11 in 100. Because the studies never have such an absence and the
extra draws come last, the research runs are identical.

**A forecast is a function of the log.** The forecasts, threat lists, causes and validity dates
were cached by `(project id, last event)`, so each demo visitor's copy of the showcase computed
the same 2,400 rollouts again; 15 visitors arriving together waited 16 s for Start and 16 s for
Pulpit. They are now keyed by a fingerprint of the log's contents (SHA-256 over each event's
type and canonical payload, plus the number of futures and the threat rule's settings), and a
result is computed once while concurrent requests for it wait (single flight). With the showcase
copied in one transaction, 15 visitors at once are served in about 5 s (34.5 s before); a second
visitor's Start and Pulpit take 0.34 s and 0.10 s instead of 2.5 s and 2.3 s.

**Fair use of a public demo.** Each visitor has a daily cap of AI requests (30) and of seconds
of audio (900; 7,200 server-wide), the live preview counted, each request at least the 10 s a
provider bills. A sandbox is made only for a client that keeps cookies (a probe cookie and a
second request), so link previews, `HEAD` requests and bots no longer create sandboxes (before:
about 15 KB and 0.5 s of CPU each). Every response carries a strict content security policy
(scripts only from the server; no inline script outside the simulator's self-contained cockpit),
`frame-ancestors 'none'`, `nosniff`, a same-origin referrer policy, the microphone for this site
only, and HSTS over HTTPS.

**The interface on every screen.** v2 was designed at 1440 and 1280 px with a horizontal scroll
below 1180 px. Below 1180 px the sidebar narrows and the Pulpit's three cards take two columns;
below 900 px the sidebar becomes a bar on top and every grid one column, with tables and tabs
scrolling in their own boxes. Visually hidden labels (screen-reader text) inside wide tables had
escaped their scroll box and widened the page; scroll boxes are now positioned. An automated
check (no element wider than its box or card, no sideways scroll) passes at 390, 768, 1024, 1280
and 1440 px on every v2 page type.

**Smaller fixes.** `/` opens v2 in the demo; the calendar link is https behind the HTTPS proxy;
the banner does not announce a new decision on the project's own Pulpit or decision page;
"Kogo dotyczy" lists the action once and the consequences; a free alternative is "bez
dodatkowego kosztu"; a long name in the Gantt chart shrinks before the word "Nieobecność".
