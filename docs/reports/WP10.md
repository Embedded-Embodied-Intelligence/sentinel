## Changes

**Before and after, per screen** (final pairs in `docs/ui/`, `pl_` and `en_`, with `_phone`):

- **Start.** Before: cards with a badge and a trend, with drafts, simulator runs and "what this is" in
  one column. After: cards led by the chance as a large number, decisions waiting first, then lowest
  chance first; quiet rows for drafts; an empty state with one primary and the examples as cards.
- **Wizard.** Before: three pages in the app shell, a team table with free-typed roles, tabs for tasks.
  After: focus mode with a stepper; a team of role rows with steppers, names and a pasted list; choice
  cards for how to add tasks; a task table with pickers for crew and predecessors; a review block.
- **Overview.** Before: stacked sections with the chance as one of many panels. After: one hero number
  with its change and a forecast strip, actions beside it, KPI tiles, decision cards, threats with
  bars, and a Gantt with a focusable column of names that fills its card.
- **Check-in.** Before: one long form of open panels. After: the AI box on top, numbered sections with
  counts and only the first open, the date read back in words, and a sticky bar with a count of the
  changes and a label that adapts ("Zapisz raport (bez zmian)").
- **Recommendation.** Before: a page in the shell with a table of options. After: focus mode where the
  page is the review: the action, the chance before and after, what changes, why in points, all
  options folded, an approve button that names the action, a reject panel that warns what comes next.
- **History.** Before: a flat filtered list. After: the chance over time on top, a segmented filter,
  entries grouped by week, details folded in place.
- **Project settings.** Before: seven panels of equal weight. After: autonomy as a ladder of four
  steps, segmented presets, costs with their explanation folded, exports with open and download, a
  danger zone at the end that confirms in place, and a bar that appears when something is unsaved.
- **Simulator.** Before: a form and a run page with side-by-side panels and a plain table. After:
  choice cards; a finished run leads with one verdict sentence, then a comparison with a difference
  column, a timeline and a Gantt; a run at work shows its progress and can be cancelled.
- **App settings.** Before: one form of provider fields. After: language, AI provider as choice cards,
  about; the key is still never shown again.
- **How it works.** Before: prose in one column. After: numbered steps, a contents list beside them
  on wide screens, the details folded.
- **Error page.** Before: a heading and a message. After: an empty-state card with what to do next.
- **Report (new, QA-08).** Before: the WP8 research cockpit. After: one self-contained page for people,
  in the application's language, with the same wording check; also for a simulation.

## Decisions
ADRs created: ADR-0027 (a design system of tokens, partials and focus mode, on the stack of ADR-0025).

- **A check-in over 7 days ahead asks for a confirmation (QA-06),** in the page only: the log is
  append-only, so a typo in the year has no way back, while the API and §11.1 stay as they are.
- **A half-filled check-in or settings form asks before leaving (QA-07),** with the browser's own
  `beforeunload` dialog; saving and Cancel do not ask.
- **A simulation at work opens a waiting page (QA-03),** which reads no run and lets the job proceed;
  Cancel stops a run at its next day, so what remains is a valid day.
- **The manager's report replaces the cockpit on the pages (QA-08);** the cockpit stays at
  `POST /api/projects/{id}/cockpit` and `sentinel dashboard`, for research.
- **The chance on a simulator page reads Sentinel's last analysis (`latest_chance`):** no new
  computation, and the same NoReaction forecast as a project's overview.
- **`scripts/qa_run.py` models the team day by day,** so that a partly present crew works at its share
  as the engine does; the first version stopped a task at any absence and made a calm project read 3 %.
- **QA logs hold no project ids (`write_log`):** they are random, say nothing, and trip the secret scan;
  the three logs of the final runs went through the same function, and only those ids changed.
- **Screenshots use a 64-colour palette** (`COLOURS`), so 80 pairs fit in 2.1 MB and the 164 QA
  screenshots in 9 MB; text stays sharp, the file is not a colour reference.
- **Problems are logged in `docs/qa/problems.md` in English,** for later WPs; the Polish report carries
  what the owner needs on a phone and links to it.

## Deviations
From the brief, where §11 or the invariants say otherwise (ADR-0027: they win):

1. **§11.7 wording kept over the brief's microcopy:** finish and bad-case labels, "jednostki kosztu",
   action titles (no "Kto" and "Za kogo" rows, no "Ludzie" row in the effects), the threat sentence,
   preset and autonomy names, "Stan na koniec dnia", "symulowane scenariusze".
2. **Not built:** the free-text "Zapytaj Sentinela" box (a new LLM role, against invariant 3 and the
   three roles) and reasons for a rejection (a new stored field, §11.5 and invariant 7). The button
   "Zapytaj Sentinela" is the existing request for a recommendation.

From the brief, for simplicity:

3. **History:** a check-in's detail is folded in place; there is no snapshot page of the plan on that day.
4. **Wizard:** names are generated per role ("Tester 1"); a name emptied by hand is still an error.
5. **Toasts** come from the `flash` parameter, not `HX-Trigger`, since most actions end in a full load.
6. **The start page** sorts by the cached chance, since nothing may block a page; cards fill in after.
7. **The Gantt's default range** is an initial scroll when the plan overflows the plot, not a range control.

From the prompt:

8. **QA-07 and QA-18 have no screenshot:** one is the browser's own dialog, the other shows nothing,
   which is the problem. The log says so.
9. **Not tested:** AI functions (no model connected), real phones (Chromium at 390 × 844), other
   browsers, the public demo. The QA report says so.

## Spec questions
Readings used; nothing in `docs/spec/` changed. Marked `# SPEC-Q` where the reading is in code.

1. **§11.4, threats: is "structural" the right filter?** A task is left out if every source–sink path
   of the required tasks passes through it. One required task with no successor ("Mapa procesów
   klienta" in run 1) gives a path around the go-live, so the go-live is no longer structural and
   tops the list ("in 9 of 10 late scenarios"), with nothing for a manager to do. Reading: as written
   (`planning.structural`). Proposal: find the structural tasks with such side tasks set aside.
2. **§11.5 item 6: where does "No change" go in the comparison?** It says alternatives are "listed by
   their own figures after the runner-up". Reading: "No change" pinned last, as the reference the
   differences are read against (`project.option_rows`), since the WP lets pages arrange things. The
   figures are unchanged. Proposal: write the pin into item 6, or remove it.
3. **Should §11.7 take any of the brief's wording?** Deviation 1 lists it. Reading: §11.7 wins.
4. **Should the simulator also show a chance that includes Sentinel's reactions?** Every chance is the
   NoReaction forecast (§11.4), so a run can read 0 % for weeks and finish on time (QA-09). Reading:
   the definition stays and the pages say "if the plan stays as it is". A second number would need
   rollouts under the policy, so it is a matter for the architect.
