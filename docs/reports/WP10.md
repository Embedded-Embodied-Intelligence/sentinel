## Decisions
ADRs created: ADR-0027 (a design system of tokens, partials and focus mode, on the stack of ADR-0025).

- **A check-in over 7 days ahead asks for a confirmation (QA-06),** in the page only: the log is
  append-only, so a typo in the year has no way back, while the API and §11.1 stay as they are.
- **A half-filled check-in or settings form asks before leaving (QA-07),** with the browser's own
  `beforeunload` dialog; saving and Cancel do not ask.
- **A simulation at work opens a waiting page (QA-03),** which reads no run and lets the job proceed;
  Cancel stops a run at its next day, so what remains is a valid day.
- **The manager's report replaces the cockpit on the pages (QA-08);** the cockpit stays at
  `POST /api/projects/{id}/cockpit` and `sentinel dashboard`, for research.
- **The chance on a simulator page reads Sentinel's last analysis (`latest_chance`):** no new
  computation, and the same NoReaction forecast as a project's overview.
- **`scripts/qa_run.py` models the team day by day,** so that a partly present crew works at its share
  as the engine does; the first version stopped a task at any absence and made a calm project read 3 %.
- **QA logs hold no project ids (`write_log`):** they are random, say nothing, and trip the secret scan;
  the three logs of the final runs went through the same function, and only those ids changed.
- **Screenshots use a 64-colour palette** (`COLOURS`), so 80 pairs fit in 2.1 MB and the 164 QA
  screenshots in 9 MB; text stays sharp, the file is not a colour reference.
- **Problems are logged in `docs/qa/problems.md` in English,** for later WPs; the Polish report carries
  what the owner needs on a phone and links to it.

## Spec questions
Readings used; nothing in `docs/spec/` changed. Marked `# SPEC-Q` where the reading is in code.

1. **§11.4, threats: is "structural" the right filter?** A task is left out if every source–sink path
   of the required tasks passes through it. One required task with no successor ("Mapa procesów
   klienta" in run 1) gives a path around the go-live, so the go-live is no longer structural and
   tops the list ("in 9 of 10 late scenarios"), with nothing for a manager to do. Reading: as written
   (`planning.structural`). Proposal: find the structural tasks with such side tasks set aside.
2. **§11.5 item 6: where does "No change" go in the comparison?** It says alternatives are "listed by
   their own figures after the runner-up". Reading: "No change" pinned last, as the reference the
   differences are read against (`project.option_rows`), since the WP lets pages arrange things. The
   figures are unchanged. Proposal: write the pin into item 6, or remove it.
3. **Should §11.7 take any of the brief's wording?** Deviation 1 lists it. Reading: §11.7 wins.
4. **Should the simulator also show a chance that includes Sentinel's reactions?** Every chance is the
   NoReaction forecast (§11.4), so a run can read 0 % for weeks and finish on time (QA-09). Reading:
   the definition stays and the pages say "if the plan stays as it is". A second number would need
   rollouts under the policy, so it is a matter for the architect.

### What the new rule does on the QA project
Run 1 again, with the same seeded team and the same chances. The largest difference is the highest late
share minus on-time share among the tasks that may be named.

| Check-in | Chance | List in 1.2.0, first task | Largest difference | List in 1.2.1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 Sep | 68 % | go-live, 9 of 10 | 10 points | two test tasks, 3 of 10 each (one after the undo) |
| 15 Sep | 73 % | go-live, 9 of 10 | 7 points | risk is spread |
| 21 Sep | 66 % | go-live, 9 of 10 | 5 points | risk is spread |
| 25 Sep | 79 % | go-live, 9 of 10 | 4 points | risk is spread |
| 30 Sep | 51 % | go-live, nearly every | 3 points | risk is spread |
| 5 Oct | 79 % | go-live, 9 of 10 | 6 points | risk is spread |
| 8 Oct | 46 % | go-live, 9 of 10 | 7 points | risk is spread |
| 13 Oct | 57 % | go-live, 9 of 10 | none above 0 | risk is spread |
| 16 Oct | 57 % | go-live, 9 of 10 | 1 point | risk is spread |

The rule does what it was asked to do: the go-live is gone. But a task named at 1 of 9 check-ins is a
list that rarely says anything, and on 8 October the page reads "the risk is spread across the plan"
right after one task grew by 4 days and a person went away, with the chance falling from 79 to 46 %. The showcase of the demo reads
the same at 39 %, with a substitute recommended on one named task. The same tasks hold the critical
chain in late and on-time futures (on 8 October the largest difference is one task at 42 % against
35 %), so lateness most likely comes from their lengths and not from which tasks they are. A threshold of 5 points would name tasks at
about 5 of the 9 check-ins. Nothing was changed on this account: the threshold is one constant,
`THREAT_DIFFERENCE` in `planning.py`, and the spec's §11.4.
