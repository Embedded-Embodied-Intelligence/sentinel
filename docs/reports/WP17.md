## Summary

The owner's audit of 1.7.0 found no failing page but ten shortcomings, mostly of the public
demo; all ten are fixed. The demo now serves 15 simultaneous visitors in about 5 s instead of
34.5 s, caps each visitor's AI requests and seconds of audio, makes no sandbox for link
previews or bots, sends strict security headers and opens v2 at `/`. v2 works on phones,
tablets and small laptops. An announced absence counts in the forecast from its first day at
once (spec v2.4.1, §11.3), while the research runs stay identical. Version 1.8.0.

## Decisions

- A forecast is a pure function of the log's contents and the number of futures, so caches are
  keyed by a SHA-256 fingerprint of the events (type and canonical payload), not by the project;
  a state without a fingerprint is never shared. ADR: none (a cache key).
- Announced absences enter through the observation and the engine (the one place every rollout
  passes), not through the disruption streams, whose answers must not depend on query order;
  the belief draws their lengths after every other draw, so worlds without them are unchanged.
- The cookie round trip instead of a user-agent list alone: a client that keeps no cookie could
  not use a sandbox anyway. Bots and link previews are also recognised by their user agent.
- On phones the theme switch and the link to v1 are hidden (the theme follows the system; v1 is
  not made for phones).

- The security headers wrap the guard (added after it), so the guard's own answers carry them
  too; found on the live demo after the first restart (the page for a link preview had none).
