# WP14 report — Status messages that understand a whole report, with dates resolved by code; voice input

- Date: 2026-11-05
- Commit(s): 30d9df3 (evaluation set, scoring script, scores of 1.4.1), 7e6aa79 (the reader,
  voice input, docs, 1.5.0), the commit of this report

## Summary

A check-in message is now read as the whole report a manager writes. Progress, a finish with its
date, a start, absences, growing tasks and new tasks each become an item to confirm, and the
confirmed tasks fill their rows of the form. The model only names each date as a calendar date
with the words it came from. Code settles the date on the project calendar and asks when it
cannot: a weekday that contradicts its date, a day off, two Mondays "od poniedziałku" can mean.
The owner's message now gives its three items: workshop done Tue 3 Nov, API in progress at 15 %,
Bartosz away from Thu 5 Nov and back Mon 16 Nov. On 30 Polish reports gpt-oss-120b gets 43 of
43 items exact with no wrong date shown without a question (1.4.1: 7 of 43, 15 wrong dates). A
microphone beside both message boxes records up to 90 s. Groq's Whisper writes it out into the
box to read and correct; neither the audio nor the text is stored. Spec v2.3.0 (§11.3 only);
ADR-0031 and ADR-0032; version 1.5.0, built as a zip, not tagged.

## Decisions

- The application has its own reader instead of extending the §10.2 parser: its schema needs
  dates and words, which the research corpus does not have (ADR-0032).
- The words of a date are limited to a closed date vocabulary and must occur in the message
  (by meaning, so a case form counts). This is the privacy guard: a valid answer is cached whole,
  so a whitelist is the only way no reason can reach the cache (ADR-0032).
- The model's date is kept when the words allow it; when the words allow one date, code's date
  wins. Words that fix nothing keep the model's date, shown unticked with a note.
- "Od <weekday>" with a present-tense verb names the Monday past or the next one: a question,
  unless a tense marker in the clause decides ("będzie", "był", "już"). The first 1.5.0 run
  showed the model taking the past Monday where the next was as likely (below).
- A day off asks for a return, a first day away, a start and a finish; an "until" day off does
  not, since "through Sunday" has the same return as "through Friday".
- Task words are matched by word forms (equal, or a common beginning of at least three letters
  and all but the last three of the shorter word); words that fit several open tasks ask, an item
  of a task already done goes to "not turned into a change".
- The review says "nieobecność od czw. 5 lis, wraca pon. 16 lis (6 dni roboczych)" rather than
  the prompt's "nieobecny": the noun form needs no grammatical gender, and the app does not guess
  it from a name.
- Ticked task items fill their rows in the browser (the row is what is saved, so a manager can
  correct it); only a review just read fills rows, so a form shown again after a refused save
  keeps the manager's edits.
- Voice: the audio as the raw request body (Starlette would spool a multipart upload to disk),
  4 MB at most, the page stopping at 90 s and the server refusing over 95 s; Groq's own key or a
  separate Groq key for recordings; no retries (a person waits); the request log's new column
  holds only the length; the settings test sends a second of a quiet tone (ADR-0031).
- The recording fixture was made with Windows' Polish voice (System.Speech, "Microsoft Paulina
  Desktop", 16 kHz mono PCM) from the text of m01; running `edge-tts` was not allowed in the
  session (external code).
- ADRs created: ADR-0031 (voice input with cloud Whisper, local Whisper as the future option),
  ADR-0032 (dates named by the model, settled by code); ADR-0028 marked partly superseded.

## Deviations

- Four expectations of the evaluation set were changed after the first 1.5.0 run (m09, m20,
  m23, m24: "od poniedziałku" with a present tense, written first as the next Monday, now a
  question). The runs before and after are scored against the corrected set; the first run kept
  its original scores (`messages-eval-after1-openrouter.json`: 40 of 43, 2 wrong dates). Each
  changed case has a `note`.
- The evaluation's "exact" treats an item shown unticked with a note as an item (type, subject
  and values must match) and a question as a question; a wrong date in an unticked item does not
  count as "shown without a question".
- `docs/spec/architecture.md` lists the modules of `app/` and `llm/` but was not edited
  (CLAUDE.md: no edit of `docs/spec/` the WP does not ask for); the new modules are
  `app/message_reader.py`, `app/date_words.py` and `llm/whisper.py`.
- Groq's Whisper is called outside the record/replay cache of invariant 3: a transcription
  makes no decision and its text is read by a person, and a cache would keep what was said
  (ADR-0031).

## Spec questions

- §11.3 v2.3.0 was written in this WP, through spec-guard, from the WP prompt's task 1. The
