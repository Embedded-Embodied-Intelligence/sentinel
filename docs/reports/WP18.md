# WP18 report — evidence registry and boundary criteria (release 1.9.0)

- Date: 2026-11-13
- Commit(s): 8366114 (registry, package, script, catalogue, map, 1.9.0), the commit of this report's final numbers

## Summary

Sentinel now has a research layer for v3: `sentinel.evidence` loads an evidence registry of 52
items for and against its design decisions, and every one of its 40 quotes (with the 8 official
Polish wordings of the EU provisions) is checked by code against the text of its fetched source;
the 6 items without a quote say why. `scripts/fetch_evidence.py` downloads the open sources
into `data/evidence/` (never committed) and runs the same check. A catalogue of 24 decisions,
six boundary criteria and a map of the twelve survey processes in three zones are derived from
the registry, and the legal dates were checked against the Official Journal. No interface or
research change; release 1.9.0.

## Changes

- `src/sentinel/evidence/`: `model.py` (Source, Verification, EvidenceItem, Registry, Decision,
  Criterion, BoundaryCell, BoundaryProcess, BoundaryMap; the rules: a verified quote has a
  source, a digest, a gloss and a location, at most three sentences, verbatim highlights, an
  English legal quote has its Polish wording, `summary_only` has a reason and no quote, own
  evidence has figures, run ids and caveats; a process takes its strictest cell's zone; the
  basis of a decision), `load.py`, `verify.py` (the documented normalisation, the quote check,
  the sentence count), `text.py` (HTML/XHTML/XML text, standard library).

  `docs/research/boundary-criteria.md`, `docs/research/boundary-map.json`.
- `tests/test_evidence.py` (10 tests, default run), `tests/test_evidence_sources.py` (`data`).
- `.pre-commit-config.yaml`: the registry joins the files full of SHA-256 digests that

- `docs/adr/0036-evidence-sources-and-quote-check.md`; `docs/spec/architecture.md` (package,

## Decisions

- One quote per item, contiguous, at most three sentences; a source with several useful
  passages became several items (E11 and E41 for Art. 14(4)(b) and (d); E27 and E45 for Raisch
  & Krakowski; E23 and E40 for OWASP LLM06). No ellipses: a quote is one exact passage.
- Highlights are exact substrings of the quote, so that the interface can mark them as they
  stand; the normalisation applies only between the quote and the source.
- An open abstract of a paywalled article counts as an open text: the abstracts of Noy & Zhang
  (Science), Lee & See and Galesic et al. are quoted from their PubMed records (the publisher's
  abstract), with `open_access: false`; no full-text passage of a paywalled article is quoted.
- EU law from the Publications Office (Cellar), which serves the Official Journal documents of
  EUR-Lex by content negotiation; eur-lex.europa.eu presented a certificate for another host.
- The basis of a decision counts only verified evidence: `empirical` (studies, ours included) >
  `normative` (laws, standards) > `theoretical`; a vendor's text counts as theory.
- Docs in English (CLAUDE.md); the texts the interface shows (`*_pl`) in Polish; the zone and
  criterion names are Polish in both.
- The decision catalogue's evidence column and the criteria's evidence lists are derived from
  the items' fields, and tests check both directions.
- ADR created: ADR-0036 (sources, pdfminer.six as a development-only extractor, the quote check).

## Deviations

- The registry has 52 items, not 35: 11 new literature, law and standard items (E36–E46) were
  needed so that every decision of task 3 has evidence and challenging evidence exists for
  Raporty (E43) and Mapa granic (E44); 6 are Sentinel's own results (E47–E52; E52, the RQ4
  autonomy study, is evidence against our own D03).
- Three items the research report marked ✔ could not be re-verified and are `summary_only`:
  E16 (the Microsoft reprint answered HTTP 403), E21 (Strathern, paywalled, no open copy), E22
  (Edmondson; Harvard DASH answered HTTP 429 twice). No site that refused the fetcher was
  worked around.
- Fields beyond the WP's list: `kind`, `language`, `highlights_pl`, `verification.reason`,
  `source_file_pl` and `sha256_pl` (the Polish wording comes from its own file), `figures`,
  `run_ids`, `runs` and `caveats_pl` (own evidence), and the registry's `sources`.
- E01 is quoted from the PubMed abstract, E04 from the published article (authors' copy), not
  from the preprints the report used; E13 quotes the contrastive thesis from the full text.
- The session ran on the owner's MacBook (Apple M1, macOS), not the WSL2 server.
