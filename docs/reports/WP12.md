## Summary
A card now promises what approving it gives: in a live project every option is worked out as the
approval applies it, at once and on the forecast's 200 worlds. So the overview after an approval
shows exactly the promised chance, most likely finish, bad case and cost (B3, B4). The chart, the
CSV and the HTML report show the current plan from the latest check-in, checked with
`validate_residual` (B2). A move of five points or more is explained by up to three one-at-a-time
counterfactuals. Polish holidays and company days off are not working days (B5). Status reports
ask before a report without progress and keep what was confirmed (B9–B16). The AI lists what it
did not understand, asks about unknown people and ambiguous tasks, explains in Polish and reads
descriptions better (B6–B8). Tasks not started can be corrected after the first check-in. Every
item of `docs/qa/raport-ceo-chrome-pl.md` has a status; all nine major ones are fixed. Spec
v2.2.0 changes §11 only; `reproduce --check` stays 117 of 117. Version 1.4.0 is built as a
zip, not tagged.

## Decisions
- B3 had two causes (measured on the showcase, the CEO's second example). First, the comparison
  imagined the approval one day later in each world, as the research rollouts do (55 %; applied at
  once on the same worlds: 59 %). Second, the next forecast re-sampled the worlds from an
  observation whose replay (§5.1) puts a handover before an earlier growth at equal work done, so
  a 2-day onboarding became 5 days (46 %). `ApprovalEvaluator` makes the card and the overview equal
  by construction; the second point is a spec question (below).
- A card shows the median and the 90 % quantile of `C`, as the overview does, instead of the mean
  (the CEO's "22 gru vs 21 gru" was mean against median).
- The plan shown is the deterministic forecast of §5.2, checked by `validate_residual` (the §4.1
  form of the §1.3 check for a plan with started work); the baseline keeps `validate_schedule`.
- Causes are one-at-a-time counterfactuals on the forecast's worlds of the check-in day. The
  progress variant keeps the check-in's absences from day `t` on: planned progress had its people,
  and otherwise the variant is refused.
- The calendar is fixed once tracking starts. Projects stored before 1.4.0 keep Monday–Friday, read
  from the stored definition's missing setting (no migration of stored days).
- A growth of a task not started revises the estimate in the definition. The default budget is
  pinned at the first check-in, so neither a revision nor a correction moves it.
- The message parser and the WBS extraction are wrapped in `app/llm.py`. The research parser,
  prompts, schemas and corpora are unchanged. The answers carry clause numbers, never message
  text, and the clauses stay in memory only (invariant 7).
- An absence whose person the message does not name (first three letters of a name part) is
  shown unticked with a note. This is the guard against `qwen2.5:7b` giving Marek's absence to
  Tomasz.
- Four research tests of 3–5 s each (`test_residual_plans_are_feasible_on_random_views`,
  `test_summaries_can_be_regenerated_through_the_cli`,
  `test_preview_shows_the_state_the_engine_leaves`, `test_runner_on_tiny_writes_every_output`)
  are marked `slow`: the default run had reached 89.3 s. They run in criterion 4b.
- ADRs created: ADR-0029 (model output a person reads may be in the interface language).

## Deviations
- "Checked by `validate_schedule` in the export path": the export path checks the residual plan
  with `validate_residual`, which is `validate_schedule`'s form for a plan with started work.
  `validate_schedule` needs an instance that holds the started work and the time-varying capacity,
  which the residual plan does not have.
- The decomposition "on the CEO's run" is shown on a run rebuilt from the report's words
  (`scripts/wp12_causes.py`): the CEO's project data are not in the repository.
- B10 cannot be timed exactly in a browser. The replay asserts that a Back during a save is never
  silent: the page either asks or the report is saved.
- Top 10 #7: the default report date stays today and is not the previous report + 7 days. A
  report is the status at the end of a day, and the future-date check would ask about every
  default.
- Corrections after tracking cover the tasks of the definition. Tasks added at check-ins are not
  corrected yet (their records are in the check-in effects).
- The friction about the shifted "Osoby" pop-up was not investigated: the report gives no steps.

## Spec questions
- **§5.1, a growth and a later handover at equal work done** (`# SPEC-Q` in
  `app/planning.py`). The replay puts a handover before a growth when both have the same work done
  (step 3 before step 5). That holds in research runs: a growth is drawn in step 5 and the task
  progresses the same day. In a live project, a growth reported at a check-in followed by a
  substitute approved at the same work done (a paused task) gets the onboarding multiplied by the
  growth factor (2 → 5 crew-member days in B3). Reading used: the replay as written; the card and
  the overview agree because both use it. Proposal: the observation keeps the order of such changes
  (an optional field, absent in research observations).
- v2.2.0 records the WP's decisions in §11 as the prompt allows: §11.1 calendar, §11.2 corrections
  and the pinned budget, §11.3 revised estimates and the message review, §11.4 plan shown and
  causes, §11.5 the live comparison and the card's dates, §11.7 wording.
